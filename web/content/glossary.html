Section 2 — Terminology Standard

Technical Glossary and Acronyms

This glossary defines the approved technical terms for the relativistic optical delay-line data storage project. All technical personnel must use these exact definitions. Do not use the unapproved synonyms in project documentation.

Approved Term / Acronym Category Symbol / Unit Approved ASD-STE100 Definition Unapproved Synonyms
ASD-STE100 Acronym N/A Aerospace and Defence Simplified Technical English standard. ste/standard, simplified/english/standard
Bit Conservation TN \(B\) [bits] The principle where the count of bits in flight equals transmitted bits minus received bits. bit/tracking, bit/storage
Cat's-Eye Reflector TN N/A An optical retroreflector made of a focusing lens and a concave mirror at the focal plane. cat-eye/mirror, cateye/reflector
Chirp Rate TN \(\dot{f}\) [Hz/s] The rate of frequency change over reception time. frequency/slew, frequency/drift
Coordinate Velocity TN \(\beta = v/c\) [-] The speed of the spacecraft relative to Earth, divided by the speed of light in vacuum. relative/speed, coordinate/speed
Corner-Cube Reflector TN N/A A retroreflector consisting of three mutually perpendicular planar mirrors. trihedral/reflector, corner/cube
Dihedral Angle Offset TN \(\delta\alpha\) [rad] The deliberate angular adjustment between mirror faces to compensate for relativistic velocity aberration. mirror/tilt, angle/bias
Diffraction Loss TN \(\eta_{\text{diff}}\) [dB] The decrease in optical power caused by the spatial divergence of the laser beam. beam-spreading/loss, divergence/attenuation
Gordon-Holevo Capacity TN \(\chi\) [bits/s] The fundamental quantum upper limit on the rate of classical information transmission through an optical channel. quantum/capacity, holevo/limit
Interstellar Medium TN / Acronym ISM The gas, dust, and cosmic rays that exist in the space between star systems. space/dust, galactic/gas
Lorentz Factor TN \(\gamma = 1/\sqrt{1-\beta^2}\) The relativistic scaling factor that quantifies time dilation and length contraction. gamma/factor, lorentz-contraction/factor
Optical Delay-Line TN N/A A transmission path that stores data in the propagation delay of electromagnetic wavepackets. light/buffer, delay/storage, optical-delay/line
Photon-Starved Regime TN N/A An operational regime where the mean received photon count per pulse or mode is much less than one. low-power/optical-link, photon-deficient/regime
Pointing Lead Angle TN \(\theta_{\text{lead}}\) [rad] The angular offset used when aiming the transmitter to hit the moving spacecraft at the retarded time. look-ahead/angle, lead/angle
Proper Acceleration TN \(\alpha\) [m/s\(^2\)] The physical acceleration experienced and measured by an accelerometer on board the spacecraft. rest/acceleration, proper/gravity
Rapidity TN \(\xi = \operatorname{artanh}(\beta)\) The hyperbolic angle parameter representing relativistic velocity. hyperbolic/speed, velocity/angle
Retarded Time TN \(t_{\text{ret}}\) [s] The emission coordinate time of a light signal that arrives at an observer at reception time. delayed/time, retardation/time
Rindler Horizon TN \(c/\alpha\) [s] The boundary in spacetime beyond which emitted light signals cannot reach an accelerating spacecraft. causal-event/horizon, acceleration/horizon
Two-Way Doppler Factor TN \(D_2 = \frac{1-\beta}{1+\beta}\) The ratio of the optical frequency received at Earth to the frequency transmitted from Earth after reflection. doppler/ratio, two-way/shift
Velocity Aberration TN \(\theta_{\text{ab}}\) [rad] The apparent shift in the direction of light caused by the relativistic motion of the reflector. light/deflection, relativistic-beam/walk-off

ASD-STE100 Compliance Rule Notice

Authors must use only approved technical names in report sections. Do not introduce unapproved abbreviations. Keep noun clusters to three nouns or fewer in all sentences.
