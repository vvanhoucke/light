Technology Readiness Assessment

Near-term precursor missions at coordinate velocities up to 0.20c use existing TRL 4 to 6 components. Extreme missions at coordinate velocities above 0.50c require major breakthroughs in TRL 1 to 3 systems. These breakthroughs include active Dihedral Angle Offset control and multi-octave quantum heterodyne receivers.
